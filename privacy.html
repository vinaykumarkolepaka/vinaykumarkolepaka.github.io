LendBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What the app stores

LendBook stores the records you enter (borrower names, phone numbers, addresses, loan amounts, payments and notes) in a database on your own phone, protected by a PIN.

Google Drive backup

If you sign in with Google, LendBook backs up these records to the hidden application data folder of your own Google Drive, using only the drive.appdata permission. The app cannot see or change any other files in your Drive. Your Google account email is used only to identify the Drive account for backups.

Sharing

LendBook has no servers of its own. It does not collect analytics, show ads, or sell or share any data with anyone. Data leaves your phone only when you back up to your own Google Drive or choose to share a statement or export file yourself.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app removes the data from your phone. You can delete the Drive backups at any time from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → LendBook → Delete hidden app data.

Google API data

LendBook's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
